Before you pay

What happens when the founding price ends?

Why can I not pay inside the app?

Google Play requires its own billing for anything bought inside an Android app, and takes a share of it. Paying here instead keeps that share with us, which is part of how these prices stay where they are.

What happens after I pay?

Your plan is active immediately. Open the Zoxia app and it is there. You do not need to enter anything into the app.

How do I cancel?

In the app, under your plan. You keep everything you have paid for until the end of the period you already paid for, then move back to Free. There is no cancellation fee and nobody to email.

Which card can I use?

Any Nigerian card, plus bank transfer and USSD where your bank supports them. Payment is handled by Paystack and we never see your card details. See our Privacy Policy.
