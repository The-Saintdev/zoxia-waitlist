Privacy Policy

This describes what Zoxia collects, why, how long it is kept and what you can ask us to do with it. It is written to be read rather than to be survived.

Last updated 6 October 2026

Who we are

Zoxia is operated by Cresco Ai LTD, a company registered in Nigeria. For the purposes of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the UK and EU General Data Protection Regulation, Cresco Ai LTD is the data controller for the personal data described here.

Write to privacy@zoxia.site about anything on this page. A person reads that address.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • We do not show your posts, or anything about them, to anyone but you.
  • We do not track you across other websites or apps.
  • This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from a third party, which is why you were not asked to agree to any.

Learning from how Zoxia is used

Zoxia gets better at the thing it is for by learning from how it is used. When enough posts have gone out, we can start to tell you useful things: which times of day actually worked for people posting what you post, which connections tend to lapse before they break, what a post that quietly failed had in common with the last one. None of that is possible from your account alone, so the learning happens across accounts.

What that means exactly, because the phrase is used loosely elsewhere:

  • We learn from our own records of how Zoxia behaved — when a post was scheduled, when it went out, which platforms it went to, whether it succeeded, and what failed when it did not.
  • We do not learn from the information the platforms give us about you or your audience. Profile details and the view, like and comment counts a platform reports are used to show you your own results and nothing else. Every platform's developer terms limit what their data may be used for, and those limits govern.
  • Anything that leaves your account is aggregated: a pattern across many creators, never your posts, your captions or your numbers attached to you.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not give it to third parties, for this or for anything else. What is learned stays inside Zoxia and comes back to you as the product working better.

If you would rather your account were left out of that entirely, email us and we will exclude it. You do not have to give a reason, and nothing about your plan changes.

The one page that is different

Everything above holds for every page of this site, including this one. The plans page is the only page that does anything at all, so it is worth saying exactly what:

  • It asks our own server for the current prices when it loads. No third party is involved and nothing about you is sent.
  • If you sign in to buy a plan, your email and password go to our server and nowhere else. Your sign-in is then held in that browser tab only, so that the page you return to after paying can tell you whether the payment landed. It is deleted the moment it is used, and it disappears when you close the tab. It is not a cookie and it is not shared.
  • Paying takes you to Paystack, who handle the payment. We never see your card details, and nothing on this site follows you there.

Your connected social accounts

When you connect TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube or X, you are taken to that platform to sign in. Your password for that platform is never shown to us or handled by us. What we receive is an access token, which we encrypt and use for three things and nothing else: to publish the posts you schedule, to read the profile details listed above, and to read how the posts we published for you did. We never read posts you made anywhere else, and we never post anything you did not schedule yourself.

You can disconnect any account at any time in Settings. Doing so deletes the token from our systems immediately. Where the platform offers a way to revoke it, we revoke it as well. Posts already published stay on that platform, because they belong to your account there rather than to us.

Our use of information received from these platforms follows their own developer terms, including the limits they set on what may be stored and for how long.

YouTube

Zoxia uses YouTube API Services to upload the Shorts you schedule and to read how they did afterwards. Because of that, a few things are worth stating plainly and in Google's own terms.

By connecting your channel you are also agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What we access, and what we do with it. When you connect YouTube we ask for permission to upload videos to your channel and to read your own channel's details and statistics. With that permission we receive your channel name, channel ID and channel picture, and for the posts Zoxia published for you, the view, like and comment counts YouTube reports. We use those to publish what you scheduled, to show you which channel a post went to, and to show you how it did. We do not read videos you uploaded anywhere else, we do not read anyone else's channel, and we never upload anything you did not schedule yourself.

How to take that permission away. You can disconnect YouTube in Zoxia's Settings at any time, which deletes the token from our systems immediately. You can also revoke Zoxia's access directly from Google, without going through us at all, on the Google security settings page.

We store the statistics YouTube reports for a post so that the record of what was published stays useful to you, and we refresh them from YouTube so they do not sit here going stale. Where YouTube's developer terms limit how long something may be held, those limits govern, and we delete rather than keep.

Who else sees it

We use a small number of services to run Zoxia. Each sees only what it needs:

  • Cloudflare R2 stores your uploads until they are published and deleted.
  • Paystack processes payments and holds the card details we never see.
  • Mailtrap delivers our emails, including your sign-in codes.
  • Expo delivers push notifications to your device.
  • TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube and X receive the posts you schedule to them, because that is the point.
  • Google, as the operator of YouTube, is who we talk to when you connect a channel. Their handling of your information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Some of these operate outside Nigeria and outside the European Economic Area. Where personal data is transferred, we rely on the safeguards those providers offer, including standard contractual clauses where they apply. We do not transfer personal data to anyone who cannot offer an adequate level of protection.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Show you the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct anything wrong.
  • Delete your account and the data attached to it.
  • Give you a copy in a portable format.
  • Restrict or object to how we use it, including the fraud-prevention data we rely on legitimate interests for.
  • Withdraw consent where we relied on it, without affecting what was lawful before you withdrew it.

Write to privacy@zoxia.site. We answer within 30 days, and we do not charge for it.

If you think we have got this wrong, you are entitled to complain to a regulator: the Nigeria Data Protection Commission if you are in Nigeria, or your national supervisory authority if you are in the EU, EEA or UK. We would rather you told us first, but you do not have to.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from Settings in the app, or by writing to privacy@zoxia.site. It removes your account, your scheduled posts, your uploads and your connected accounts. The full detail is here.

Security

Access tokens for your social accounts are encrypted at rest. Passwords are hashed and cannot be recovered by us. Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. No system is perfect, and we will not pretend otherwise: if a breach affects your personal data we will tell you and the relevant regulator as the law requires.

Children

Zoxia is not for children. You must be at least 18 to use it, which is also what the platforms we publish to require of their own developer integrations. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a child has an account, write to us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and for anything that materially affects you we will tell you in the app or by email rather than quietly editing the page.
