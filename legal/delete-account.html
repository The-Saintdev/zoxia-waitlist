Delete your account

Two ways to do it, and a plain list of what goes. No retention offer, no survey, no "are you sure" three times over.

Last updated 13 September 2026

In the app

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account under Account.
  3. Confirm. That is the whole thing.

By email

Write to privacy@zoxia.site from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm when it is done, and within 30 days at the latest.

We ask you to write from the account's own address because it is the simplest way to be sure the request is really yours. If you have lost access to that mailbox, tell us and we will find another way to establish it.

What is deleted

  • Your account, name, email address and password hash.
  • Your profile picture and bio.
  • Every scheduled post, and every video and photo still stored for one.
  • Every connected social account and its access tokens.
  • Your notification history and your device's push token.

What is not, and why

  • Posts that already published. They live on TikTok, LinkedIn or X and belong to your account there. Delete them on the platform itself; we have no way to reach back and remove them for you.
  • Records of payments. Tax and company law requires us to keep financial records for a period, so the fact that a payment happened stays. It is not linked to anything you upload, and we never held your card details in the first place.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription on its own. Cancel the plan first, in Settings, or write to us and we will do both together. We would rather say this here than have you find out from a card statement.

Disconnecting one platform instead

If you only want Zoxia to stop publishing to one place, you do not need to delete anything. Open Settings, tap the connected account, and disconnect it. The access token is deleted straight away and revoked with the platform where that is possible.
