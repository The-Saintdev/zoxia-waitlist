Support

There is no ticket queue and no chatbot. These addresses reach people who work on Zoxia.

Last updated 13 September 2026

Where to write

support@zoxia.site Something is not working: a post that did not go out, an error you cannot get past, a bug
hello@zoxia.site Anything else: a question about plans, an idea, or getting in touch
privacy@zoxia.site Your data: a copy of it, a correction, a deletion, or a complaint

We answer within two working days, and within 30 days for anything about your data, which is what the law requires of us.

If a post did not publish

Open the post in Zoxia first. Every destination carries its own status and its own reason for failing, written to be acted on rather than to be forwarded to us. Most failures are something you can fix in a minute: a connection that expired, an account set to private, a caption too long for one platform.

If it is not one of those, write to support@zoxia.site and tell us:

  • The email address on your account.
  • Roughly when the post was meant to go out, and your timezone.
  • Which destination failed, and what Zoxia said about it.

Please do not send us your password, and do not send us a code from a sign-in email. We will never ask for either, and anyone who does is not us.

Using Zoxia from ChatGPT or Claude

You can connect Zoxia to an AI assistant and schedule from a chat: "post this to TikTok on Friday at 7pm". Zoxia publishes it at that time whether or not the chat is still open, and the post appears in your queue in the app like any other.

Connecting

In Claude, open Settings, then Connectors, then Add custom connector. Name it Zoxia and paste this address:

https://api.zoxia.site/mcp

In ChatGPT, Zoxia is on its way to the app directory. Once it is listed you will find it under Apps, and connecting takes one tap.

Either way you are taken to a Zoxia page to sign in and choose what the assistant may do, and then back again. Zoxia never sees your ChatGPT or Claude account, and they never see your Zoxia password.

What an assistant can do

Connecting lets it see your connected accounts and your posts, check what is left of your plan, and schedule posts. Scheduled posts wait in your queue, where you can still change or remove them yourself.

Two things are asked for separately, and both start switched off:

  • Publish straight away, without waiting for a scheduled time.
  • Edit or cancel posts, including ones you scheduled yourself.

To change your mind, disconnect and connect again with different switches. You can disconnect at any time in the app, under Settings, then ChatGPT and Claude. It takes effect immediately.

Things worth knowing

  • An assistant cannot connect or disconnect your TikTok, LinkedIn or X accounts, change your plan, or read your password.
  • TikTok posts still need you to choose who can see them. The assistant has to ask you.
  • Posts to X use your X allowance, whether you or an assistant schedules them.
  • Everything an assistant does is recorded, so support can tell you what happened and when.

A few things that come up

Why does my TikTok post go out as "Only me"?

TikTok restricts apps that have not finished their review to posting privately. Until that review completes, every post Zoxia publishes to TikTok goes out visible only to you. You can change it to public in TikTok itself afterwards. Zoxia says so before you schedule rather than after.

Why was my caption refused for one platform but not another?

Each platform has its own ceiling, and a post going to several has to satisfy all of them. X allows the least by a long way. Zoxia checks this in the composer and names the platform that refused it, so you can either shorten the caption or drop that destination.

Why do extra X posts cost money when the others do not?

Because X charges us for each one, and TikTok and LinkedIn do not. Rather than raise every plan to cover a platform most people do not use, X is sold as a dearer version of each plan with a stated allowance, and extra posts can be bought singly.

Can I get my data?

Yes. Write to privacy@zoxia.site and we will send you a copy of what we hold, free, within 30 days. The privacy policy lists what that is.
