What do you do?

Every scheduler says it posted. Most only know that they sent it.

Handing a post to a platform and having a platform publish it are two different events. Between them sit expired connections, duplicate rules, copyright holds and region blocks. If nobody checks, the calendar says it went out and the account says otherwise, and you find out when a client asks.

What Zoxia does today

One post, three platforms, one upload.

Write it once and send it to TikTok, X and LinkedIn together. The video uploads a single time, not once per destination.

Each destination confirmed separately.

After a post is sent, Zoxia goes back to the platform and asks what happened to it. A post is only marked published when the platform says so, and a refusal names the destination and the reason.

TikTok confirmed, LinkedIn confirmed, X refused

A warning before a connection dies.

LinkedIn drops your connection every 60 days and will not renew it on its own. Zoxia warns you three times before it goes, so a week of scheduled posts does not quietly stop.

reconnect LinkedIn: 48 hours, 24 hours, 6 hours
