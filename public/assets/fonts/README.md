# Fonts

Geist and Geist Mono, by the Geist Project Authors (Vercel), under the SIL Open
Font License 1.1. Full licence in `OFL.txt`, upstream at
https://github.com/vercel/geist-font

The `.woff2` files here are the latin and latin-ext subsets, taken from the
Google Fonts CDN. They are self-hosted rather than linked so the page makes no
third-party request and the visitor downloads about 52KB instead of the full
family. `latin-ext` only downloads when a glyph needs it, which on this page
means the naira sign.

Sizes stay small on purpose: the people this page is for pay for their own data.
